---

## Scenario

For this project, you will have the freedom to select a dataset of your choice to perform an independent data analysis. The goal is to demonstrate your ability to find, clean, analyze, and visualize data, all while communicating meaningful insights. This open-ended project allows you to explore data that aligns with your personal interests, career goals, or curiosities.

## Task

Find a dataset from a reputable source, conduct a thorough analysis, and present your findings.

### Where to Find Data
Here are a few suggestions on where to find interesting datasets for your analysis:

- [Kaggle Datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets): A wide variety of datasets across industries and topics.
- [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/): A comprehensive search engine for datasets across various sources.
- [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/ml/index.php): A repository of datasets for machine learning and data analysis.
- [Data.gov](https://www.data.gov/): Datasets from the US government on various topics like climate, education, and healthcare.
- [Awesome Public Datasets](https://github.com/awesomedata/awesome-public-datasets): A collection of public datasets from around the web.

## Specifications

1. **Dataset Discovery**: 
   - Select a dataset that interests you. The dataset should have enough complexity and size to perform meaningful analysis (recommendation: at least 1,000 rows).
2. **Data Exploration**:
   - Discover and visualize your dataset to gain insights. Include key visuals and written conclusions.
3. **Data Cleaning**:
   - Discuss your approach to cleaning the data (handling missing values, outliers, and data types).
4. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - Conduct statistical analysis, report on relevant statistics, and describe any meaningful patterns you find.
5. **Outliers & Missing Data**:
   - Explain how you identified outliers and missing values, and describe what you did with this information.

## Deliverables

1. **Jupyter Notebook:**
   - Submit a comprehensive Jupyter notebook containing your analysis. Ensure the notebook is well-organized with a combination of code cells and markdown cells. Use markdown cells for explanations, interpretations, and context, and include comments in your code. The notebook should be clear and easy to follow.

2. **Visualizations:**
   - Include a minimum of 7 visualizations in your Jupyter notebook. Use different types of visualizations (bar charts, scatter plots, histograms, etc.) that provide insights into your dataset. Ensure all visuals are clearly labeled with captions and titles.

3. **Presentation:**
   - Prepare a 5-7 minute presentation summarizing your key findings. The presentation should highlight important visualizations and tell the story behind your data analysis. Save the presentation as a PDF and add it to your repository.

4. **README.md:**
   - The README.md file should provide an overview of the dataset, the steps taken in your analysis, and your key conclusions. It will serve as a guide for users to understand your project.
